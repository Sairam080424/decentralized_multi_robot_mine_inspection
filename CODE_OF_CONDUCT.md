# Code of Conduct

## 1. Our Commitment
We are committed to providing a respectful, professional, and collaborative environment for all contributors, maintainers, and reviewers working on the Decentralized Multi-Robot Mine Inspection Platform.

## 2. Expected Standards
- Demonstrate empathy, professional courtesy, and respect toward team members.
- Provide constructive, evidence-based feedback on technical designs and pull requests.
- Prioritize system safety, software reliability, and rigorous engineering ethics.
- Encourage knowledge sharing and open collaboration across all robotics disciplines.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior
- Demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing language and conduct.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Disregard for established architectural safety rules and review gates.

## 4. Enforcement & Reporting
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior. Concerns or incidents should be reported to the platform lead maintainers.
